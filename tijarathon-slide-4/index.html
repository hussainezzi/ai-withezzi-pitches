VC Stress-Test: The "Scaling Pods"

The Original Idea

Hire more Pods (1 PM + 2 AI Orchestrators) as orders come in. At 1 Lakh/mo per Orchestrator, a pod costs ~400k PKR/month.

The VC Critique

"This is a bloated agency model. You need 17 clients per pod just to cover salaries. If humans scale linearly, margins will bleed and cashflow dies on churn."

The VC Solution
